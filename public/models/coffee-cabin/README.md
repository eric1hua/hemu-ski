# Coffee cabin

`coffee-cabin.glb`: Building 7 on the summit shoulder (`src/world/summit.js`, `cabin()`), a Tripo-made log cabin with a steep gabled shingle roof, a little spire, a coffee-shop interior (bar, tables, stools, plant, ceiling lamps) and a guitar-playing figure on the porch. Exported with Blender 5.2 headless from `output/cabin-windows/log-cabin-game-lit-v05.blend` (ignored, local; v05 is the one with the lamps and lights re-added) by the asset lab's `scripts/export-coffee-cabin.py`: each part's modifiers baked first, so the Boolean window cuts go through the walls, then the hidden cutters and the camera left out, the 230 `tripo_part_*` meshes joined into one, the lamps, window frames and glass kept as their own meshes, the 5 `Interior_Light_*` point lights exported (KHR_lights_punctual), the 4096 px base colour halved to 2048 JPEG. SHA-256 `3ad5c7c7d2f4fbf50442967743ec6e789b3a13d9bed6c614a17f2fdadda0c180`, 3.8 MB. The file also carries Blender's default `Light` (54k cd, no range); `summit.js` ignores it. (The export before this one dropped the cutters before baking, so its walls were whole behind the glass and the windows looked solid.)

About 1.0 × 0.95 across and 0.78 tall (spire included), centred on x/z with its base at y = 0, front and open door facing +z. The interior floor stands 0.042 up. The room inside the walls runs x −0.42 to 0.436 and z −0.25 to 0.16, with an alcove for the tall window at the back (to z −0.38); the front wall is z 0.16–0.25, its doorway x −0.09 to 0.136 and about 0.31 high, the ceiling 0.33. Out in front are ledges under the windows, the guitarist and the door, opened outward. The bar stands 0.106 above the floor, the long table 0.08, the chairs' seats 0.045.

`summit.js` shows it 10 times its size (10 m wide, 9.5 deep, a 3.1 m door): a 1.06 m bar, a 0.8 m table and 0.45 m seats beside a 1.8 m rider. It's sunk so its floor sits on the snow (the pad there is level), with the lamps' light scaled to match (`CABIN_LIGHT`). It swaps materials for the toon ones (the glass keeps the file's colour and 0.18 opacity, the lamps go unlit warm). Collision boxes (`CABIN_SOLIDS`) follow the walls round the doorway and the furniture, ledges, guitarist, open door and post box, so riders and Mantou walk in and round the bar and tables rather than through them. Mantou lives inside (`cabinSpace().home`), going in and out by a spot just inside the door and one out in front of it, and the ride camera comes in close behind you indoors (`indoors` in `src/main.js`).
