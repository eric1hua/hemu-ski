# Prayer wheels

`prayer-wheels.glb`: one set of the summit's prayer wheels, object 3 (`src/world/summit.js`, `prayerWheels()`; three sets stand in a row), a Tripo-made rack of seven gilded wheels between red posts under a white-tiled roof with gold eaves. From the Tripo export `chinese+bells+3d+model.glb` (SHA-256 `38889e7d134abd69086c5c4956ff6a5ec38b92897d23a9e0be092c1fbbe94b31`, 4.3 MB), kept in the asset lab as `03-tripo-exports/prayer-wheels-v01/`. The lab's `scripts/export-prayer-wheels.py` rewrote it without Blender, so the meshes and the 2048 px base colour went over byte for byte. It named the nine parts `roof`, `frame` and `wheel_1` to `wheel_7` (along +x), and dropped the normal and metallic-roughness maps, which the game's toon shading doesn't read. SHA-256 `cbd4f316b171eca1d899996250ff84b37f032321a67388e241540382e7c01b5f`, 1.8 MB, 11,142 triangles.

About 0.98 long (x) by 0.26 deep (z) and 0.526 tall, centred on x/z with its base at y = 0. Front and back are alike, so it faces either way; the game turns its +z side to the path. The posts stand at x ±0.448 and the frame is 0.05 deep. Each wheel is about 0.09 across and 0.162 tall, standing from 0.136 to 0.298, with the wheels 0.107 apart. Each wheel mesh sits a little (up to 0.003) off its node, and the game re-centres it so it turns on its own axle. The roof's eaves are at 0.344 and reach out to z ±0.129.

`summit.js` shows it 5.5 times its size: 5.4 m long and 2.9 m tall. The wheels are 0.5 m across and 0.89 m tall, standing from 0.75 m to 1.64 m, their middles level with the walking rider's shoulders (1.2 m). The eaves are at 1.89 m, just over her head (1.84 m); any smaller and her head would go through them. It swaps the material for toon ones, the wheels with a rim of light. Its collision box runs post to post, as deep as the posts (0.3 m), which lets a rider within 0.4 m of the wheels' face, close enough to put a hand on them, and leaves the roof overhead. `src/world/prayer-wheels.js` turns the wheels.
